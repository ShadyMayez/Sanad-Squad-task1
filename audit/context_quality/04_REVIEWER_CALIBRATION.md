# Reviewer calibration

The rubric now distinguishes six independent dimensions: factual support, technical correctness, clarity, usefulness, brand voice, and platform format. Accurate paraphrasing can earn factual support; copying cannot establish entailment by itself. A supported but repetitive thread or bare checklist post can receive low usefulness. The Reviewer is told to use the current draft and neighboring pinned context, never an earlier rejection or an unsupported causal explanation.

Thresholds remain factual support and technical correctness at least 4, all other scores at least 3. Deterministic errors, any adverse atomic verdict, and invalid provenance remain mandatory vetoes regardless of score. Rejection feedback should identify the precise present-tense defect and the source limitation. The Reviewer does not rewrite text or approve publication on its own.
