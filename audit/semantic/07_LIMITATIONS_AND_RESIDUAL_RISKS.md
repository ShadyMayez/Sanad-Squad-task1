# Scientific limitations and residual risks

The source snapshots prove only what their exact bytes say. The SWC Registry explicitly warns that its content has not been thoroughly updated since 2020; some examples describe older Solidity compiler behavior. The KB scope fields and verifier instructions require version conditions, but they do not update old guidance. The two OWASP source pages were matched to the official OWASP SCS repository and its CC BY-SA 4.0 license; attribution and license copies are in `source_snapshots/`. ShareAlike obligations should be followed when redistributing adapted snapshot text. Source URLs and snapshot hashes establish provenance, not the truth of every sentence or current applicability.

Atomic extraction is deterministic sentence and conjunction segmentation. It covers every alphanumeric public span or reports an error, but it can over-segment a sentence or miss an implicit proposition, rhetorical implication, pronoun reference, or unstated premise. Writer mappings provide candidate evidence only; they are not treated as proof. A stronger semantic parser with human-checked calibration data would improve claim boundary quality.

`qwen3.5:4b` is a small local model. Its semantic judgments can be wrong despite structured output, exact span selection, and the skeptical veto. The fixed benchmark has only two supported and 15 adverse semantic cases; zero observed false accepts is not a guarantee. It also confused CONTRADICTED with INSUFFICIENT_EVIDENCE on four cases. The skeptical second pass uses the same model and therefore has correlated failure modes. Human review remains necessary before publishing, particularly for technical mitigation claims and version-dependent advice.

The verifier checks pinned quotes, not the full surrounding source or independent secondary sources. A clipped quote may omit a crucial condition. No live web refresh is implemented. The 8,192-token context window and at-most-25-claim bound avoid unbounded requests; large inputs may drop after a structured-output or context failure rather than silently truncate evidence. Model and Reviewer decisions are recorded, but raw Ollama generation traces and per-slot queue wait time are not.

The visual output is a written specification, not an image. It is content-aware within a small deterministic topic pattern set; an unsupported visual mechanism is excluded by conservative fallback wording. Expand and editorially test patterns before broad platform use. The CSV is a draft export and does not authorize publishing.
