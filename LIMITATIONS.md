# Known limitations and next steps

- Python checks pinned snapshot hashes, exact source quotations and claim mappings. A local model makes per-claim semantic judgments, with a second skeptical pass for tentative support. This remains an audit aid, not formal proof. A human should review exported copy before publication.
- The relation-aware gate now blocks the demonstrated SWC-107 recommendation-to-root-cause inversion and obvious internally contradictory positive decisions. Its syntax patterns do not understand every causal paraphrase, cause/effect reversal, or multi-span inference. A model can still misclassify a relationship despite two passes, so accepted public claims need technical editorial review.
- The current 4B writers may paraphrase and reorganize supported information, but the model can still add unsupported causality or lose a condition. The atomic verifier, skeptical pass, Reviewer, and Python gates reduce that risk without eliminating it. An editor should review factual scope and style before use.
- On the final 60-case verifier replay, two supported recommendations were rejected; the fresh 20-case holdout rejected two of ten supported claims. The final standalone technical thread also dropped on a disputed paraphrase. These are real false-negative and model-variance costs of the conservative gate, not evidence that every adverse claim will always be caught.
- The new 20-case relation set exposed worse positive recall: 5/10 supported claims in its first execution and 4/10 in a final-code replay. Valid source-attributed recommendation recall was 0/4 then 1/4. The same fixed input produced different model decisions across runs. Until that is corrected and independently re-evaluated, do not treat the pipeline as reliably producing publishable material.
- The offline KB stores attributed source snapshots and URLs. Runtime does not fetch live pages or verify current web availability. The SWC Registry states that its content has not been actively maintained since 2020; refresh older entries against maintained security guidance before production use. OWASP snapshots carry CC BY-SA 4.0 attribution and ShareAlike obligations documented in `source_snapshots/`.
- Topic matching is lexical. Unsupported or ambiguous focuses fail their slot; a future version could add a verified retrieval index with human review of matches.
- Visual output is a written specification, not an image. A scheduler expecting media needs a separate conversion step.
- Only X-specific character and thread rules are implemented. Additional platforms need their own deterministic validator and output contract.
- **Supported Recall Yield Limitation:** Overall supported recall on the 64-case frozen held-out benchmark achieved 82.35% (28/34), missing the predeclared 85% target by 2.65%. While false acceptance remained strictly 0.0% (0/30) and precision was 100%, conservative verification on the 4B parameter model still generates false rejections on complex multi-span synthesis and subtle mechanism paraphrases.
- **Historical Applicability Scope:** Weaknesses in legacy SWC entries—such as constructor misnaming in SWC-105—apply to historical compiler versions (Solidity < 0.4.22) where constructors shared the contract's name. Modern Solidity (0.4.22+ and 0.5.0+) enforces the `constructor` keyword. Version-specific claims must be qualified to legacy Solidity environments to avoid misrepresenting obsolete compiler behaviors as current vulnerabilities.
- **Instrumental Mechanism Boundaries:** The pipeline now strictly decomposes compound causal clauses (`since`, `because`) and vetoes ungrounded instrumental assertions (`by <action>-ing`), ensuring that separate recommendations (such as aggregation and outlier rejection in OWASP-SC03) cannot be falsely coupled as mechanism and outcome. However, open-ended domain prose can still contain subtle causal nuances beyond the comprehension of a 4B local model, necessitating pre-publication expert security review.
- **Evidence-First V2 Catalog Scope:** In Evidence-First V2 (`--mode evidence_first_v2`), content generation is strictly bounded by the curated proposition catalog (`knowledge/evidence_first_v2/catalog.json`). Uncataloged topics abort at the sufficiency preflight gate (`INSUFFICIENT_EVIDENCE`) to prevent ungrounded generation. Expanding coverage requires human curation of canonical propositions and evidenced relations.
- **Local Workstation Concurrency Boundary:** Ollama local inference is strictly clamped to `max_concurrency = 1` via semaphore to prevent VRAM exhaustion and inference failure on ~16 GB RAM / 4 GB VRAM workstations.
- **Local Qualification vs Publication Clearance:** Local qualification demonstrates deterministic compliance, plan conformance, and adversarial resistance under local testing. Commercial deployment requires human editorial sign-off, which can be enforced via `BusinessPolicy.human_review_requirements.require_human_approval_for_ship = true`. When human approval is required, content is routed to `ready_for_human_review.json` and strictly excluded from `posts.csv` until an authorized human signs off.
- **Catalog Grounding and Modality Preservation:** Curated propositions must strictly mirror snapshot modalities (e.g. "sometimes" vs "often") and compiler applicability boundaries (e.g. legacy Solidity <0.4.22). Moving factual decisions into a structured catalog ensures consistency, but inaccurate catalog propositions or relationships will be reproduced faithfully. Deterministic preflight conformance checks now veto modality escalation, universal scope overclaims ("all Solidity code"), and missing version qualifiers.
- **Single Small-Model Conformance Fallibility:** A 4B local model (`qwen3.5:4b`) cannot be trusted alone as an unconstrained semantic judge for subtle epistemic nuances. Deterministic preflight checks must act as a hard barrier before model evaluation.
- **Topic Isolation and Writing Guidance Cleanliness (Material Blocker A Remediation):** Generic planner guidance must never contain topic-specific mechanisms or foreign vulnerability concepts (e.g. legacy constructors or initialization logic in a reentrancy thread). Writing guidance must strictly derive from the assigned unit's proposition and relation IDs. Deterministic cross-topic concept vetoing (`topic_signatures`) in `conformance.py` acts as a fail-closed safety barrier against prompt contamination.
- **Fail-Closed Plan and Policy Authority Binding (Material Blocker B Remediation):** Plans must bind explicitly to active policy ID, policy version, and policy hash, rather than relying on unvalidated defaults. In `publication_eligible` mode, the catalog and live graph strictly require approved proposition and relation statuses (`APPROVED`), failing closed on any pilot-only entries (`REVIEWED_FOR_PILOT`).
- **Cryptographically Bound Human Approval Record (Material Blocker C Remediation):** Boolean approval flags (`human_approved = true`) do not constitute an authoritative clearance decision for regulated workflows. When business policy requires human sign-off (`require_human_approval_for_ship = true`), content requires an authenticated `HumanApprovalRecord` binding approver ID, role, timestamp, approved draft hash, policy ID, and policy version. Unsigned or mismatched drafts fail closed and remain in `ready_for_human_review.json`.
- **Structured Output Grammars vs Natural Language Prompts (Closure 01):** Local small language models (`qwen3.5:4b`) can infer a 1-to-1 mapping between canonical propositions in a content unit and tweet array elements if the JSON output contract allows unbounded array lengths (`min_length=1` without `max_length=1`). Enforcing strict bounds (`min_length=1, max_length=1` for marketing posts; `min_length=5, max_length=10` for technical threads) directly in the Pydantic schema and JSON schema grammar guarantees structural compliance before generation, completely eliminating avoidable format-induced slot drops.
- **Unit-to-Proposition Mapping and Writer-Verifier Alignment (Closure 02):** Bundling multiple disparate mechanisms or consequences into a single content unit creates ambiguity for both the Writer and Conformance Verifier on small models. When the Writer distributes bundled propositions across separate tweets, the verifier can misalign tweets against unit expectations. Structuring content plans with clean 1-to-1 proposition-to-unit mapping ensures deterministic alignment across generation and verification.
- **Semantic Model Fallibility on Plausible Domain Extrapolations (Closure 02):** Small local models frequently rationalize unapproved domain-plausible extrapolations (such as asserting fund draining for a DoS vulnerability, or ranking multi-source aggregation as the "primary defense") as mere "stylistic" or "logical" extensions. Pure prompt instruction is insufficient; layered deterministic invariant checks for comparative rankings, hyperbolic severity, and unlicensed outcome domains must gate model conformance.
- **Actionable Marketing Recommendation Constraints (Closure 03 / F2):** While `check_marketing_action` robustly distinguishes actionable security recommendations from passive vulnerability descriptions, lexical pattern matching cannot fully understand arbitrary semantic rephrasings of security advice. The writer is therefore guided toward standard actionable framing (`Review...`, `Inspect...`, `To mitigate/prevent this, isolate/verify...`).
- **Secondary Witness Scope for Concrete Implementation Tooling (Closure 03 / F4):** While OpenZeppelin `ReentrancyGuard` and `Ownable` are standard industry implementations of the lock and ownership mechanisms described in SWC-107 and SWC-105, they represent specific realization choices. In strict production regimes where every library reference must be backed by a primary authority witness, adding dedicated OpenZeppelin repository documentation records to the KB/catalog avoids reliance on general domain convention.
- **Instrumental Mechanism Conflation (Closure 04 / B1):** Small models can conflate distinct vulnerability remediation mechanisms—such as attributing SWC-104's execution-continuation behavior to SWC-113's failure-isolation pattern ("to prevent execution from continuing on failure"). Layered deterministic semantic conformance checks now detect and veto unlicensed instrumental mechanisms (`prevent/stop/halt execution from continuing on failure`) for failure-isolation units.
- **Universal Scope Overgeneralization (Closure 04 / B2):** Small language models frequently expand scoped recommendations (such as SWC-104's requirement to check return values for low-level call methods) into universal assertions ("for all external calls"). Layered deterministic conformance guards now explicitly detect and veto universal scope overclaims (`all (external )?calls`), enforcing strict scoping to low-level call sites.
- **Residual Model Generation Variance:** Even with dual role-specific instructions, deterministic brand/platform gating, and one-revision ceilings, local 4B model generations can occasionally vary in stylistic phrasing across seeds. Pre-publication human review remains mandatory for all publicly deployed content.
- **Editorial Wording vs Factual Certainty (Final Release 01):** Small models naturally gravitate toward absolute causal language (e.g. "To prevent these outcomes...") or ungrammatical predicate blends when combining multiple guidance directives. Explicit prompt instructions for qualified risk reduction ("To reduce this risk...", "To mitigate these risks...") and complete natural grammar must be maintained alongside deterministic conformance gates.

- **V3 Live Web Research Operational Boundary (`IMPLEMENTED_WITH_LIVE_RESEARCH_BLOCKER`):** The live research provider (`LiveResearchProvider`) and security fetcher (`SafeFetcher`) are fully implemented with strict SSRF defense (blocking private IPv4/IPv6, loopback, and cloud metadata `169.254.169.254`), domain allowlist gating, redirect checking, and zero-credential audit logging. However, in environments where external search API credentials (`TAVILY_API_KEY`) are not provisioned or internet connectivity is isolated (`--network none`), the pipeline runs deterministically in `fixture` mode using verified allowlisted fixtures.
- **Single-Tweet Marketing Post Limitation:** Under the V3 Marketing Guide contract, `marketing-post` pieces are strictly constrained to 1 tweet ($\le 280$ characters). The multi-tweet marketing thread format (`MarketingFormat.THREAD`, 3–7 tweets) is defined and reserved in schema, but strictly blocked in execution until multi-tweet marketing contracts are authorized.
- **Third-Party Accretion Audit Findings Isolation:** Third-party audit reports (such as the 20 Accretion findings) are strictly isolated as external reference evidence and never claimed as SanadSquad audits. The canonical firm audit history `audits.json` is strictly maintained as an empty array (`[]`) validating against `schemas/audits.schema.json`.
- **Deterministic Human Writing Quality Heuristics:** Code gates deterministically enforce human writing rules (banning em dashes, separator en dashes, curly quotes, emoji, 14 buzzwords, negative parallelism, copula overuse, filler openers, and repetitive rule-of-three phrasing). While these gates eliminate stereotypical AI markers, subjective stylistic polish still benefits from human editorial review.
- **Company Contact Configuration & Approval Boundary (`company-contact.json`):** Commercial marketing CTAs containing company contact details require an approved, non-empty `company-contact.json` configuration (`approved: true`). In the baseline repository, official contact details have not yet been supplied by the project owner; therefore `company-contact.json` is safely maintained with `approved: false` and `status: "AWAITING_OWNER_APPROVAL"`. The deterministic validator fail-closed blocks any public emission of contact details when unapproved. Contact details are strictly metadata for business inquiries, never factual evidence supporting technical claims.
